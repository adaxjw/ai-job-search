# Behavioral Profile

## Overview
Ada's behavioral assessment (Hogan-informed, self-reported) identifies a pattern of **high ambition paired with low power/status drive**: strongly motivated to achieve and grow, but not motivated by hierarchy or title for its own sake. Combined with high curiosity/learning orientation and low process orientation, she is best suited to fast-changing, ambiguous, innovation-friendly environments rather than rigid, process-heavy hierarchies.

## Core Behavioral Drives

| Drive | Level | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| Ambition | High | Strongly driven to achieve, grow, and take on greater scope |
| Power/Status Drive | Low | Not motivated by hierarchical status or title for its own sake; motivated by substance and impact |
| Curiosity / Learning | High | Energized by new domains, continuous learning, and novel problems |
| Process Orientation | Low | Prefers ambiguity and fast iteration over rigid procedure or bureaucracy |

## Strongest Behaviors
- **Ambition + curiosity:** Seeks roles with genuine room to grow and learn, not just climb the ladder
- **Comfort with ambiguity:** Thrives building net-new initiatives from scratch (China Sustainability Strategy at VW, China Innovation Incubator at Audi, largest AI-assisted GTM launch at Elsevier)
- **Motivated by substance over status:** Explicitly seeks "real decision rights" and design authority, not just a senior title

## How You Work Best
- Fast-changing, ambiguous, innovation-friendly environments
- Environments where curiosity and continuous learning are actively rewarded
- Low-bureaucracy settings with light process overhead and fast decision cycles
- *[Inferred from LinkedIn About]* — motivated by "building things that are thoughtful, enduring, and human"

## Growth Areas (frame positively in applications)
- **Low process orientation:** Frame as a preference for designing efficient processes rather than defaulting to inherited ones. Back this with concrete evidence of building structure when it adds value (e.g., the VW 37% cost optimization, EY performance systems and execution roadmaps) rather than avoiding process altogether.

## Mapping to Job Posting Language

When a job posting mentions these keywords, it's a **strong behavioral fit**:
- "ambiguous", "0-to-1", "fast-paced", "entrepreneurial", "build from scratch", "cross-functional", "innovation", "transformation", "strategic ownership"

When a job posting mentions these, flag as **potential friction** (not deal-breaker):
- "highly structured process", "strict SOP adherence", "hierarchical approval chains", "status-quo maintenance", "execution-only", "reporting line several layers from leadership"

## Management Style Preferences
- Works best with **active sponsorship** and **real decision rights**, not purely advisory scope
- Prefers leaders who grant autonomy and design authority over close oversight
- **Doesn't work:** structurally isolated roles, remote from the decision-making centre, with no path to influence direction

## Using This in Applications
- **Cover letters:** Lead with curiosity- and ambition-driven achievements (net-new initiatives, transformation work), not generic "team player" language
- **CV:** Emphasize scope of decision-making and design authority, not just delivery volume
- **Interviews:** Use STAR examples that show initiative taken in ambiguous, undefined situations
- **Don't overstate:** Avoid implying a love of process/structure for its own sake — be honest that structure is built only when it serves speed or impact
